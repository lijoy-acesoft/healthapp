How HealthApp Works

Personalized.
Proactive.

A single platform that delivers personalized health insights for individuals, workforce health intelligence for companies, and data driven risk assessment for insurance partners.

01
Connect Your Health Data

Seamlessly integrate data from leading wearables, fitness devices, health apps, and medical records. HealthApp brings your health information together in one secure platform, making it easier to understand and manage your overall well being.

  • Centralized health tracking across devices, apps, and health records
  • Automatic data syncing with no manual entry required
  • Real time activity, vital signs, lifestyle, and laboratory data
  • Secure data protection with enterprise grade privacy control
02
AI-Powered Health Analysis

HealthApp evaluates more than 100 health parameters, including vital signs, lifestyle habits, medical history, and mental well being. Advanced analytics convert complex health data into an easy to understand Health Score and risk profile.

  • Analysis of cardiovascular, metabolic, hormonal, and kidney health markers
  • Monitoring of blood pressure, heart rate, cholesterol, glucose, BMI, and more
  • Dynamic Health Score that updates as health data changes
  • Integrated mental wellness assessment alongside physical for a true 360° analysis
03
Expert-Guided Personalized Care

Health insights are reviewed using evidence-based medical frameworks and supported by an expert clinical panel led by Dr. Brian Rotenberg, MD, MPH, FRCSC. Users receive personalized recommendations designed to improve health outcomes and support long term wellness.

  • Personalized recommendations based on individual health profiles focused on long-term well being
  • Practical and expert-reviewed recommendations for nutrition, fitness, sleep, and lifestyle improvement
  • Proactive illness prevention to reduce medical costs over time
  • Continuous progress tracking and wellness improvement support
04
Workforce & HR Intelligence

Companies gain access to workforce health dashboards that provide visibility into organizational health trends, helping leaders make informed decisions, strengthen employee wellness programs and build data-driven wellness policies.

  • Monitor workforce health trends across teams and departments
  • Identify emerging health risks and potential cost drivers
  • Support preventive wellness initiatives and employee engagement
  • Reduce administrative effort through centralized reporting and analytics
05
Intelligent Insurance Risk Assessment

HealthApp helps insurance partners evaluate risk using current health data and structured health records. The goal is faster, more consistent underwriting and better alignment between risk profiles, premiums, and coverage options.

  • Health based risk scoring using current health data
  • Personalized recommendations that encourage healthier outcomes
  • Improved underwriting efficiency through structured health records
  • Better alignment between risk profiles, premiums, and coverage plans

Privacy Built Into Every Step

HealthApp is built to protect sensitive health information through strong privacy controls, secure data management, and transparent access permissions. Users maintain control over how their information is shared while organizations and insurance partners access only the data relevant to their role.

  • Choose what information is shared and who can access it
  • Customizable privacy settings tailored to individual preferences
  • Secure storage and transmission of sensitive health information
  • Role based access for individuals, companies, and insurance partners
  • Transparent policies that support trust, accountability, and compliance
